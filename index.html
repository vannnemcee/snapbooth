ABOUT SNAPBOOTH

Designed like a modern photography studio, powered purely by web standards.

SnapBooth brings the tactile charm of physical analog photobooths to any browser—without servers, external uploads, or tracking. Every frame is captured and composited locally on your device.

01

Direct MediaDevices Capture

Connects directly to front, rear, or external USB cameras with real-time aspect ratio framing, mirror calibration, and precision countdown timers.

02

6 Bespoke Canvas Layouts

From 35mm perforated film stock and obsidian darkroom contact sheets to editorial cream grids and commemorative event posters.

03

Non-Destructive Darkroom

Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, color grade presets, frame stock colors, titles, and date stamps with instant live preview.

04

Print-Calibrated Output

Dedicated print stylesheet isolates your high-resolution photostrip and scales it proportionally on paper with zero UI chrome or cropping.
